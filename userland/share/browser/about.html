About Orange Browser

Orange Browser is WPE WebKit 2.54 built for OrangeOS: WebKit's engine, JavaScriptCore, libsoup and OpenSSL, running in OrangeOS processes and drawn into a Peel window.

  • Rendering: Skia on the CPU, into shared memory.
  • JavaScript: JavaScriptCore with its JIT. Code pages are never writable and executable at once: they are written through a second mapping.
  • Network: HTTPS with Mozilla's trust store in /etc/ssl/cert.pem.
  • Tabs: Ctrl+T opens one, Ctrl+W closes it, Ctrl+Tab moves between them.
  • Cookies and site data are kept on the data disk (/data) and survive a restart.
  • Video and sound: GStreamer with FFmpeg's decoders (H.264, VP9, AAC, Opus), including the streaming YouTube uses. Decoding runs in software, so high frame rates stutter under emulation.
  • Not yet: WebGL, protected (DRM) video, AV1.
